Working demo

Your picture books, read on your own site

Families buy the ebook in your Shopify shop, exactly as they buy the printed book today. Then they read it here, one page at a time. Try it: the email and code are filled in for you.

  1. 1
    Sign in with the order emailNo new password. The reader checks your shop for a paid order with this email.
  2. 2
    Enter the one-time codeSent to that email, so only the buyer can open their books.
  3. 3
    See only the books they boughtOther titles show as locked, with a link back to your shop.
  4. 4
    Read one page at a timeEach page is a picture of your printed page, so the artwork and layout stay exactly as you designed them. Every page carries the buyer's order number. The book file itself never leaves our server.

What no reader can stop: a screenshot, or a phone camera pointed at the screen. The order number on each page shows whose copy it was.
